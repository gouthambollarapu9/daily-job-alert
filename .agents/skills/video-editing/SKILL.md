# Video Editing

1. Inspect source footage and confirm runtime, aspect ratio, and output.
2. Follow exact timecodes when supplied.
3. Build the cut around the story and usable audio, then handle continuity, pacing, grade, and captions.
4. Check caption timing, dialogue intelligibility, and exports at the intended size.
5. Where a source edit affects people, preserve facial identity and structure.
