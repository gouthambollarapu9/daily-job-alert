# Ad Creative

1. Identify the audience, offer, objective, placement, and CTA.
2. Develop distinct concepts with a hook, visual direction, copy, and CTA for each.
3. Carry approved product packaging, logos, prices, and claims into every variation.
4. Make required disclosures legible and verify spelling, safe zones, and offer terms before export.
5. For reference-based people, preserve facial structure and identity while adapting pose, lighting, and background.
