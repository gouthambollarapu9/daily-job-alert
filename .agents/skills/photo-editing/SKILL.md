# Photo Editing

1. Use the supplied image as the identity and product reference.
2. Apply only requested changes.
3. Preserve facial structure, proportions, and distinctive features while adapting pose, lighting, or background when requested.
4. Keep skin texture natural and verify packaging and logos against the source.
5. Inspect at full size for halos, distortions, and unwanted changes before exporting to the requested dimensions.
