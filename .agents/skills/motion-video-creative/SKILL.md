# Motion Video Creative

1. Build a timed storyboard with a start state, movement, and end state for each shot.
2. Specify camera direction, screen direction, timing, typography, transitions, and audio cues.
3. For generated shots, identify which objects stay fixed and where moving elements enter, pass, or exit.
4. Preserve facial identity and structure across reference-based shots.
5. Verify continuity, on-screen copy, and the final hold at playback speed.
